## 2. Public money

**FZOEU, Fond za zaštitu okoliša i energetsku učinkovitost.** The Croatian fund for environment and energy efficiency. Confirmed:
- Announced in July 2026 a new call this autumn for citizens, about 20 million euro for energy efficient vehicles and also small chargers. Source: https://www.tportal.hr/no-planet-b/clanak/fond-za-zastitu-okolisa-na-jesen-objavljuje-poziv-za-energetski-ucinkovita-vozila-20260707
- Its earlier vehicle programmes included electric mopeds and cargo vehicles, about 11,000 vehicles co-financed. Source: https://informator.hr/vijesti/fond-za-zastitu-okolisa-na-jesen-objavljuje-poziv-za-energetski-ucinkovita-vozila
- A call for alternative fuel vehicles for businesses, EnU-4/26, funded by NextGenerationEU through the NPOO. Source: https://www.fzoeu.hr/docs/267/Javni%20poziv%20za%20kupnju%20novih%20vozila%20na%20alternativna%20goriva%20EnU-4-26.pdf
- Croatia's Social Climate Plan reserves about 974 million euro for sustainable mobility in the next period (stated by the ministry, same tportal source).
Relevance: high. Check whether an NGO or a company set up by the NGO is an eligible applicant for e-mopeds and chargers.

**EU LIFE Programme, Climate Change Mitigation.** The 2026 call (LIFE-2026-CLIMA-SAP-CCM) closed on 22.9.2026. Source: https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/funding-opportunities/calls-proposals/climate-change-mitigation-0_en
Relevance: medium, the next round normally opens in spring with a deadline in September, so 2027 is the realistic target. LIFE projects are large and usually co-funded, typically 60 percent. A consortium would help.

**Clean Energy for EU Islands Secretariat.** European Commission initiative for the more than 2,200 inhabited EU islands. Croatia signed the 2020 memorandum. The Croatian NGO **Pokret otoka (Island Movement)** has been a regional partner of the Secretariat. Sources: https://www.sei.org/projects/clean-energy-for-eu-islands/ and https://smart-cities-marketplace.ec.europa.eu/node/3226
Relevance: high as a partner and door opener, more than as direct money. The C4T Groundwork technical assistance call for island authorities closed 11.9.2026: https://clean-energy-islands.ec.europa.eu/news/eu-islands-invited-apply-technical-assistance-accelerate-clean-energy-transition

**Also worth checking (not verified this session):** Interreg Italy Croatia (cross border, tourism and mobility), EIT Urban Mobility, local tourist boards (turističke zajednice) of each island, and town and municipality budgets for udruge.

## 4. Car-accessible islands (starting list from general knowledge, verify)

**By bridge:** Krk, Pag, Čiovo, Murter, Vir, Pašman (bridge from Ugljan).
**By car ferry:** Cres, Lošinj (bridge from Cres), Rab, Ugljan, Dugi otok, Iž, Brač, Hvar, Šolta, Vis, Korčula, Lastovo, Mljet.
Pelješac is a peninsula and is out. Check every one against current Jadrolinija car ferry lines.

A natural first pilot is Ugljan (Kukljica), where Baba has deep ties and knows the ground. Suggest it, do not assume it.
