# NANO BANANA PROMPTS — Green and Go Croatian Islands

Seven images for the pitch page. Use the same style line on all of them so they look like one set. Landscape 16:9. No text and no logos in the images, the page adds the words.

Save them as `01_hero.png`, `02_ferry.png` and so on, and upload them to the next chat in this order.

## 01 hero, the container on the island

```
Photorealistic wide shot of a renovated 40 foot shipping container converted into a small rental office on a Croatian Adriatic island. The container is painted a soft pine green with warm wooden cladding around a large glass counter window, a fabric awning, a small wooden deck with two potted olive trees, and a neat row of six electric seated mopeds plugged into chargers along its side. Solar panels on the roof. Behind it, old Dalmatian stone houses, pine trees and a calm turquoise sea. Early morning golden light, clear sky, quiet and inviting. No text, no logos, no people. 16:9.
```
